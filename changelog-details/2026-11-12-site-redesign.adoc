= 2026-11-12 — Site redesign

The product site at `apps/site` is the public homepage for userdata.cloud while the apex domain still serves the organization Pages site.

* Visual system: Sora, a violet action color, a green seal, and one violet rail beside the home headline. Light, dark, and auto live in the top bar and on the appearance settings page.
* News: the 12 November 2026 organization note is published on this site. Docs link to `https://docs.userdata.cloud/home/`.
* Sign-in stays a browser session marker. A signed-in visit shows a circular picture (or initials) that opens Settings and Sign out.
* Public copy: a folder is one storage host. The record is a structured document the issuer signed, and this site is not the archive.
* The theme icon is in the first HTML response. CSS shows the sun or the moon from the theme attribute set before paint.
* Sora Latin is inlined in `apps/site/src/fonts/sora-latin.css` (`font-display: block`). Regenerate with `node apps/site/scripts/bake-sora.mjs`.
* Header and favicon use `apps/site/public/mark.svg`, the symbol only. The placeholder wordmark was outlined type in the source file and is not in the site mark. The Vecteezy credit is on `/about`.
* The header name is `userdata`. `data` uses inlined Doto regular (`apps/site/src/fonts/doto-regular.css`). Regenerate with `node apps/site/scripts/bake-doto.mjs`.
* On load, a second `data` starts just to the right. The colors are `rgb(91, 77, 255)` and its additive complement `rgb(164, 178, 0)`. `plus-lighter` makes the overlap white, and the pair is differenced with the page so that white reads as ink. The group has no fill, so the header does not show a plate behind the word.
* `data` is `1rem`, the same size as `user`.
* The home hero is a violet band with space under the buttons. A green band below carries lines such as “Records that stay on a host of your choice.” The footer is only News, docs, and about.
* A fixed field of 0s and 1s sits behind the page and fades out to the page background. The home bands are translucent over it.
* Sign in uses `.btn--signin` (seal outline). `.btn--primary` stays on Open your vault and downloads.
* Settings are stored in `localStorage` under `prv_preferences_v1`. They cover account, identity, vault prompts, mail ingestion, household members, notifications, privacy, appearance, expected issuers, the browser companion, a developer note, and this browser's session. Withholding identity still drops the open vault from the tab.
